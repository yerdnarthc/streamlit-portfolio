# My Autobiography

I have dedicated myself not to use AI here for my autobiography so that my life experiences will be delivered raw and genuine. As someone who spends hours debugging code ***(or rather let the AI agents do the debugging?)***, prompt-engineering, and watching algorithms reshape our world, choosing to start writing on a blank page over an LLM really feels like an act of rebellion. However, AI can't feel the intricate emotions and feelings that I've evoked from my life challenges and wins, nor do they remember all the different events on my life that brought me to where I currently am today. Hence, this is my story, that is unfiltered, un-optimized, and written entirely by a human.

Born on the 1st of September, 2005, and raised in Cebu City, Philippines, I am Arth Andrey C. Endrina. We are three siblings in the family, of which I'm the eldest one. I am just from an ordinary, humble family. My mother's name is Ma. Elena C. Endrina, and she is from Asturias, Cebu. My father's name is Arthur J. Endrina, and he is from Mati, Davao Oriental. 

I don't have that much of memory on my very early childhood. The only thing that I'm reminded of is that back then, I was a very hyperactive, restless little kid who always caused trouble. I unfortunately skipped nursery because my parents really wanted me to learn more about the usual basic and fundamental lessons in every early child's phase, and so therefore I started my first education directly to Kinder 1. I still have a vivid memory of myself memorizing counting from 1 to 1000 at around age 4 or 5. Unfortunately though, I have experienced my very first major accident early in my life. Back when I was still 4, myself, including my father and mother, suffered a serious motorcycle accident when we were on the way to our province at some part in Cebu Transcentral Highway. Thankfully though, all of us survived, but it had left me a deep scar on the side of my eye that to this day, is still visible. However, at the end of all of it, I'm really still thankful enough to God that all of us survived it and have healed over time. I'm also thankful that this accident didn't caused me any severe trauma up until now.

Moving forward to my middle childhood, I took my elementary education at SAE YOUNG Chirstian School. That school was very cool because it was there that I saw foreigners, specifically Koreans, more personally, since the school is owned by a Korean. I still remember where every month of August, some group of Korean people will visit the school to celebrate the Dental Month and they gave away good-quality toothbrushes and toothpastes from South Korea, and also offer free dental services for all of us students in the school.

By around year 2018, our family decided to move from Cebu City to Lapu-Lapu City because we have found a more comfortable living place there. For this reason, my elementary education was split into two. I took Grade 6 elementary there in Lapu-Lapu City, and I met there new people and new friends. Over time, I have adjusted to the difference on my school life in Cebu City to Lapu-Lapu City. Some of my notable achievements in this point include me participating the City Palaro event as an athlete and earning several places on 100-meter dash, Long jump, Shot put, 500-meter relay race as a group, and Javelin throw. I also attempted to participate in Prescon competitions but unfortunately wasn't successful because I was unexpectedly hit with a flu on the day of the competition.

In my high-school days, Junior High School was by far for me, the best school memory that I've ever had and the most memorable. Almost all of my classmates from my batch in Grade 6 was still my similar classmates all throughout Junior High School, and we have no difficulty on bonding all around each other. There are many funny, embarrassing, and victorious moments packed in these days. This is the point in my life where I'm most active in terms of participation and competition too. Earning places in competitions like Math Sayaw as a class section, Garbo sa Lapu-Lapu as a class section too, Jazz Chants as a class section too, Spoken Poetry, Copy Reading English Category in Prescon, and more.

During Senior High School days, it's a time of another environment-adjustment, because transitioning from JHS to SHS was a real thing. New classmates again, new faces, new school, and new environment. I took SHS on University of Cebu - Lapu-Lapu and Mandaue. I have also a notable achievement here, and it was that I managed to place 2nd place in the whole school for the Sudoku competition. It's something that's maybe a little bit niche, but something that I can be proud of myself. This here is the point that I started to decide for my college course. Funny enough, Computer Science was absolutely not the course that I've chosen right away. My first choice initially was Civil Engineering. Then, I took an interest in some "computer techy" courses, which I've then decided to pursue Computer Engineer instead. However though, the "engineer" element in the course name didn't convinced me enough to just stay on the course, and that's where I switched my attention fully to Computer Science. Just how this course sounds cool alone have fully convinced me and my entire life, that maybe this is where I belong and this course is for me, and that's what I've sticked with till college finally came in.

Then, here comes College, which is by far have the most hard-hitting transition I've ever experienced. It's like everything is new and an unexplored territory for me. The first subjects that were taught in Computer Science was really fun for me. It had left me an impression that made me think, "Wow, so this is how fun Computer Science feels like, huh?". Never did I know that I was so wrong in thinking it like that. Second (2nd) year and now (3rd) year made me question my decisions and my abilities. But despite of how it seems like hard and challenging this course is, the important thing for myself is that I still manage to get everything done and pass, not stopping and giving up on even one, and still continues to pursue this.

Computers really fascinate me, and personally, I'm enjoying my stay at Computer Science despite its level of difficulty, and I think that's what matters more. Currently, I am very happy on where I am in my life today, and I am looking forward to what the future holds for me. Since then, I've been developing a lot of projects, either school/required-projects, like this website where you're reading right now, or side-projects of mine, and it's been a lot of fun really. I want to continue growing, learning, adapting, and honing my skills inside this course. It's only a matter of time and there's only one year left for me in this course, but it seems to feel like the journey to the end is still ahead away.

This is my life.
